## 💰 Business Models

Choose 1-2 models that fit your goals:

### Option 1: SaaS (Software as a Service) ⭐ _Recommended for beginners_

**How it works:**

- You host the backend (AWS/GCP/DigitalOcean/Railway)
- Customers access via web/mobile apps
- Charge monthly subscription

**Pricing tiers:** $29-199/month based on:

- Number of documents
- API calls/queries per month
- Storage limits
- Team seats

**Pros:**

- ✅ Recurring revenue
- ✅ You control infrastructure
- ✅ Lower barrier for customers
- ✅ Easier to update/maintain

**Cons:**

- ❌ Hosting costs
- ❌ Support responsibility
- ❌ Uptime requirements

---

### Option 2: Self-Hosted License

**How it works:**

- Sell the codebase with a license key
- Customer hosts on their own infrastructure
- You provide documentation + limited support

**Pricing:**

- One-time: $500-5,000
- Annual license: $200-1,000/year

**Best for:** Enterprise clients with strict data requirements

- Healthcare (HIPAA)
- Finance (SOC2)
- Legal (confidentiality)
- Government

**Pros:**

- ✅ Higher upfront revenue
- ✅ No hosting costs
- ✅ Appeals to security-conscious buyers

**Cons:**

- ❌ Harder to update customers
- ❌ Support complexity
- ❌ Piracy risk

---

### Option 3: White-Label Solution

**How it works:**

- Partners (agencies/consultants) rebrand and resell
- They sell to their clients under their own brand
- You provide the technology backbone

**Pricing:**

- Setup fee: $2,000-10,000
- Monthly platform fee: $200-500/month
- Or revenue share: 20-30%

**Best for:** Scaling without direct sales

---

### Option 4: Open Core Model

**How it works:**

- Open-source the core functionality (community version)
- Sell premium features as paid add-ons

**Premium features to sell:**

- Advanced analytics dashboard
- SSO/SAML authentication
- Priority support
- Custom integrations
- Multi-tenant support
- White-labeling

**Examples:** GitLab, Supabase, n8n

## 🛡️ Legal & Protection

### Business Structure

- [ ] Form an LLC (or equivalent in your country)
- [ ] Open business bank account
- [ ] Get business insurance (optional but recommended)

### Legal Documents Needed

1. **Terms of Service** - What users agree to
2. **Privacy Policy** - How you handle data
3. **License Agreement** - For self-hosted sales
4. **Refund Policy** - Clear expectations

**Tip:** Use templates from [Termly](https://termly.io) or hire a lawyer for $500-1000

### Protecting Your Code

For self-hosted licenses:

- Implement license key validation
- Obfuscate critical business logic
- Use legal agreements with clear usage terms
- Consider compiled binaries for sensitive parts
- Watermark code with license info

### Licensing Options

| License          | Best For              | Restrictions                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Proprietary**  | Maximum control       | No code sharing allowed                   |
| **Dual License** | Open core model       | Free for open source, paid for commercial |
| **AGPL**         | Forcing contributions | Must share modifications                  |

## ⚡ Quick Wins to Increase Value

Before selling, consider adding these features:

### High Impact, Low Effort

- [ ] **Admin dashboard** - User management, usage stats
- [ ] **Usage analytics** - Query counts, popular documents
- [ ] **Export features** - Download chat history, reports
- [ ] **Branding options** - Custom logo, colors

### High Impact, Medium Effort

- [ ] **Multi-tenant support** - One instance, many customers
- [ ] **SSO integration** - Google, Microsoft login
- [ ] **Webhooks** - Integrate with other tools
- [ ] **API rate limiting** - Tier enforcement

### Enterprise Features

- [ ] **SAML/OIDC** - Enterprise SSO
- [ ] **Audit logs** - Compliance requirement
- [ ] **Role-based access** - Admin, user, viewer
- [ ] **Data residency options** - EU, US servers
